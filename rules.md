# Rules
These are the rules for my journal:

## Consistency
- I must write in the journal at least once every day.
  - If I miss one or several, I can continue and just acknowledge it in my next entry.
  - If I know I will miss one or several, I can exempt myself by stating how many I plan to miss in my last entry.

## Entries
> Note: New entries are added at the top of the file

Entries follow this format:
```
## Entry <entry-#>: <entry-title>
`timestamp: <YYYY-MM-DD>`

<entry>
```
- `<entry-#>`: which number entry this is. The first is 1, then it is +1 for every new entry.
- `<entry-title>`: a title for this entry. Entries can share a title.
- `<YYYY-MM-DD>`: the date this entry was made on.
- `<entry>`: the actual entry. Feel free to have fun with this (e.g. ASCII art).

## Privacy
> Note: This is not an exhaustive list

Things I may share:
- My first name
- The country I'm in

Things I will not share:
- My full name
- My date of birth
- My address, suburb or city
- Specific locations I visit
- Other people's names or private contact details
- Details about my school, work, or education

## Honesty
I will not lie in entries, and that includes the timestamp.
I am allowed to not say the full truth for whatever reason, but I will not lie.
